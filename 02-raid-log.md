# NovaLend RAID Log

Legend: **R** = Risk, **A** = Assumption, **I** = Issue, **D** = Dependency

| ID | Type | Description | Owner | Mitigation | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Risk | NovaWallet API slips again past its committed date, blocking the credit-scoring data feed NovaLend needs for the CBN report. | Backend Lead (delivery) / SM (escalation) | Build against a contract-tested mock from Sprint 1, Day 1 (see `06-dependency-map.md`); weekly dependency sync; escalate if slip exceeds 3 days. | High |
| R2 | Risk | Team morale stays low through a high-pressure 6 weeks, leading to disengagement or a third missed sprint. | Scrum Master | Root-cause retro before re-committing to a plan; visible small wins in Sprint 1; weekly 1:1 check-ins; protect Review/Retro ceremonies even under time pressure. | High |
| R3 | Risk | Compressed testing window (QA leave in Sprint 2 + freeze in Sprint 3) lets a regulator-facing defect reach the CBN submission. | QA Engineer (test design) / SM (schedule protection) | Shift-left testing before QA's leave; reserve the last 2 days of Sprint 3 exclusively for regression on the report path; PO accepts reduced feature scope to protect this time. | High |
| R4 | Risk | The remote contractor's 5-hour timezone gap causes decisions to be made without their input, creating rework or resentment. | Scrum Master | Ceremony redesign with a fixed overlap window and a written decision log (see `05-ceremony-redesign.md`); no scope decision is final until the contractor has had a chance to weigh in. | Medium |
| R5 | Risk | Compliance/Risk review takes longer than the squad assumes, since their turnaround time is outside the squad's control. | PO / Compliance liaison | Engage Compliance in Week 1 with the draft report format, not Week 6 with the finished build; agree an explicit review SLA up front. | High |
| A1 | Assumption | The CBN deadline date is genuinely immovable and scope, not the date, is the only flex variable. | PO | Confirm explicitly with Head of Digital Factory and Compliance in the Week 1 stakeholder update; if this assumption is wrong, the whole plan structure needs rework. | High |
| A2 | Assumption | The pre-approved leave belongs to the QA engineer, 5 working days, in Sprint 2 (Weeks 3–4). | Scrum Master | Confirm actual name and exact dates with the team/HR on Day 1; adjust the capacity plan immediately if incorrect. | Medium |
| A3 | Assumption | The public holiday in this window is Nigeria's Independence Day (1 October), falling in Sprint 3. | Scrum Master | Confirm against the actual 2026 public holiday calendar in Week 1; low impact to fix if wrong, but affects the capacity chart. | Low |
| I1 | Issue | Two consecutive sprints have already been missed, and the root cause hasn't been formally diagnosed yet. | Scrum Master | Run a dedicated root-cause retro in the first 48 hours of Sprint 1, before committing to a new sprint goal. | High |
| I2 | Issue | No mock or contract test double currently exists for the NovaWallet API — integration testing today only happens against their live, unreliable environment. | Backend Lead | Build a mock/stub service against the last known API contract by end of Sprint 1, Week 1. | Medium |
| D1 | Dependency | NovaLend's alternative credit-scoring engine depends entirely on NovaWallet's transaction-history API — this is the critical path for the CBN report. | Backend Lead + NovaWallet Squad Lead | Weekly dependency sync, mocked contract for parallel development, formal escalation path if the dependency slips (see `06-dependency-map.md`). | High |
| D2 | Dependency | Final CBN submission requires Compliance/Risk sign-off, which sits entirely outside the squad's control and has no agreed turnaround SLA today. | PO / Compliance liaison | Proactive Week 1 engagement with draft format and explicit SLA request, rather than waiting until the build is finished. | High |

**Notes for the panel:** Severity reflects both likelihood and consequence given the hard deadline — anything rated High is either on the CBN critical path or affects the team's ability to function under sustained pressure. This log is a living document; it's reviewed and updated at every sprint planning session, not written once and filed away.
