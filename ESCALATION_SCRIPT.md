> "I want to flag a deadline risk while there's still time to act on it, not after it's too late to matter. NovaLend's CBN report depends on an API from the NovaWallet squad that is now \[X] days delayed with no firm new date. Our current plan protects the deadline by \[specific mitigation — e.g., 'continuing on our mock and absorbing the delay into Sprint 3's buffer'], and as of today I believe the \[date] deadline is still achievable. If the API isn't in staging by \[specific trigger date], that will change, and I'll come back to you immediately with what the revised options are rather than waiting for the deadline to arrive. For now, this is a heads-up, not an ask for a decision."
